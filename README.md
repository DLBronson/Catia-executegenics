Hello Mastercam,

Our customer Puget Sound Naval Shipyard purchased a Mastercam post processor through Ellison for a DN Solutions DNM 6700 VMC with FANUC controller.
The machine also has a Samchully 4th axis rotary table.

We do not normally bundle CAM post processors with a new machine purchase, but in this case we did. (We also did for another case at approximately the same time, for a VTR1620M machine - but my question in this case relates to the DNM post, specifically).

The post processor purchase occurred long enough ago that it was through and fulfilled by your (then) distributor, MLC CAD.

The Navy shipyard received the DNM machine itself at approximately the same time as MLC CAD delivered a post processor (late 2025/early 2026).
It has taken many months for the customer to supply power to the machine, and then coordinate installation of the equipment and accessories.
We have an Applications Engineer on-site today and tomorrow providing DNM Machine Operator Training.

My questions are:
